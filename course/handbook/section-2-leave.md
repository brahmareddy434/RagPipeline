# Acorn Labs Employee Handbook v3.2 — Section 2: Leave

*Fictional training document. Handbook version v3.2, effective 2026-01-01.*

**2.1 Annual leave allowance.** Employees receive 20 paid leave days per year.

**2.2 Planned leave notice, two years of service or more.** An employee with two or more
completed years of service must submit planned leave requests at least 5 working days in
advance.

**2.3 Planned leave notice, under two years of service.** An employee with less than two
completed years of service must submit planned leave requests at least 10 working days in
advance. This longer notice period applies for the whole of the first two years and is not
reduced by prior approval.

**2.4 Emergency leave.** Emergency leave can be requested on the same day by informing the
reporting manager.

**2.5 Carry forward.** Unused leave can be carried forward to the next year, up to a
maximum of 5 days. Carried forward days are not a new annual allowance.

**2.6 Sick leave.** The company provides 10 days of paid sick leave every year.

**2.7 Jurisdiction interaction.** Where a jurisdiction annex in Section 9 sets a longer
notice period or a higher minimum allowance than this section, the annex applies.
