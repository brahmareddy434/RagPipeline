# Acorn Labs Employee Handbook v3.2 — Section 5: Conduct

*Fictional training document. Handbook version v3.2, effective 2026-01-01.*

**5.1 Reporting harassment.** Employees may report harassment to HR or any manager.

**5.2 Manager conflict.** If the reporting manager is involved in a harassment complaint,
contact HR directly.

**5.3 Immediate danger.** Report immediate physical danger to building security and local
emergency services.

**5.4 Laboratory footwear.** Wear closed-toe shoes in the laboratory. Open-toe footwear is
not allowed in the laboratory.
