# Acorn Labs Employee Handbook v3.2 — Section 9: Jurisdiction annexes

*Fictional training document. Handbook version v3.2, effective 2026-01-01.*

Annexes apply only to the jurisdiction named. Acorn Labs employs staff in four
jurisdictions: IN, UK, DE and US. No annex exists for any other jurisdiction.

**9.1 India (IN).** The statutory minimum paid leave in IN is 18 days per year, which is
below the Section 2.1 allowance, so Section 2.1 applies in full. Planned leave notice
follows Section 2.2 or 2.3 according to length of service.

**9.2 United Kingdom (UK).** The statutory minimum paid leave in UK is 28 days per year
including public holidays, which is higher than Section 2.1, so UK employees receive 28
days. Planned leave notice in UK must be at least twice the length of the leave requested,
and never less than the Section 2.2 or 2.3 minimum.

**9.3 Germany (DE).** The statutory minimum paid leave in DE is 24 days per year, which is
higher than Section 2.1, so DE employees receive 24 days. Any single absence of more than
10 consecutive working days in DE additionally requires works council approval.

**9.4 United States (US).** There is no statutory minimum paid leave in US, so the Section
2.1 allowance of 20 days applies in full. Planned leave notice follows Section 2.2 or 2.3.
