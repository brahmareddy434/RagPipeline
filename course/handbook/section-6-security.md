# Acorn Labs Employee Handbook v3.2 — Section 6: Security

*Fictional training document. Handbook version v3.2, effective 2026-01-01.*

**6.1 Suspicious email.** Report suspicious emails to security@acorn.example and do not
open their attachments.

**6.2 Paper documents.** Confidential paper documents must be shredded in the secure
disposal bins.

**6.3 Access error ERR-4032.** For access error ERR-4032, request VPN group membership
through the IT help desk.

**6.4 Passwords.** Never share passwords with colleagues or visitors.

**6.5 Visitors.** Visitors need host approval and a reception badge and must remain with
their host.
