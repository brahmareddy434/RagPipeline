# Acorn Labs Employee Handbook v3.2 — Section 7: Benefits

*Fictional training document. Handbook version v3.2, effective 2026-01-01.*

**7.1 Bicycle repair allowance.** The organization provides a bicycle repair allowance of
40 credits per month.

**7.2 Claiming the allowance.** The bicycle allowance requires a repair receipt submitted
to People Operations.

**7.3 Not covered.** This handbook contains no policy for dental premiums, gym
subscriptions, car parking charges, or commuter rail subsidies.
