# Acorn Labs Employee Handbook v3.2 — Section 4: Expenses

*Fictional training document. Handbook version v3.2, effective 2026-01-01.*

**4.1 Reimbursable expenses.** Approved business travel and office supplies are
reimbursable expenses.

**4.2 Submission deadline.** Employees must submit expense claims within 30 calendar days
of purchase and attach itemized receipts.

**4.3 Payment.** Approved expense claims are paid in the next monthly payroll.

**4.4 Exclusions.** Personal entertainment expenses are not reimbursable.

**4.5 Rejected claims.** For a rejected claim, contact finance@acorn.example with the
receipt and claim identifier.

**4.6 Project ORCHID-77 override.** For project ORCHID-77 only, the travel expense
submission deadline is 14 calendar days after purchase. This project-specific deadline
overrides the general deadline in Section 4.2. Itemized receipts are still required.
