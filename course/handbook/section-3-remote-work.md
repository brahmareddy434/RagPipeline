# Acorn Labs Employee Handbook v3.2 — Section 3: Remote work

*Fictional training document. Handbook version v3.2, effective 2026-01-01.*

**3.1 Weekly limit.** An employee may work remotely for up to 2 days per week with manager
approval.

**3.2 Notice.** Planned remote work must be requested at least 1 working day in advance.

**3.3 Emergency remote work.** During an emergency, an employee may request remote work on
the same day by contacting the reporting manager.

**3.4 Security duties.** Remote workers must use the company VPN and must not share their
account credentials.
