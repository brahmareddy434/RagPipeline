FICTIONAL TRAINING POLICY FOR ACORN LABS
Employees receive 20 paid leave days per year.
Employees must submit planned leave requests at least 5 working days in advance.
Emergency leave can be requested on the same day by informing the reporting manager.
Unused leave can be carried forward to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days.
The company provides 10 days of paid sick leave every year.
